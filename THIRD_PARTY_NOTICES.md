and [Apache license](licenses/ipfs/LICENSE-APACHE) are preserved. Source: https://github.com/ipfs/kubo/tree/v0.43.0.

## llama.cpp assistant runtime

llama.cpp (release b12584) is bundled from its official macOS arm64 release under the MIT license.
Its [upstream license](licenses/llama/LICENSE) is preserved unchanged. Source: https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/tree/b12584.
