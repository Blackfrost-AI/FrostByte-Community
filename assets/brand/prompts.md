# Artwork prompts

Tool: built-in imagegen (no CLI/API fallback).

## App icon

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: production macOS app icon for Frost/Byte, a desktop application for sharing and downloading AI model files. Primary request: one exceptionally polished, original app icon. Canvas square 1024x1024. A single dark graphite rounded-square macOS-style tile, centered and occupying about 84 percent of the canvas with generous transparent outer margins and fully transparent corners outside the tile. Center an iconic bold angular FB monogram: interlocking geometric F and B forms, subtly suggesting an ice-cut crystal and two connected peers. Integrate one crisp diagonal negative-space slash into the mark as a nod to the name Frost/Byte. The symbol should feel intelligent, fast, independent, and slightly underground, but highly refined. Electric lime #22D511 is the main mark color, with a restrained pale icy green facet highlight for dimension. Tile is near-black #101710 with subtle bevel and immaculate material lighting. Strong silhouette, very few broad shapes, no thin details. The monogram must occupy most of the tile and remain recognizable at 32 pixels. Straight-on orthographic view, no perspective tilt. Style: premium contemporary desktop software icon, minimal sculptural emblem, precise geometry, subtle depth instead of a busy illustration. No text, no tiny lettering, no words, no border around the canvas, no surrounding scene, no multiple variants, no generic snowflake, no circuit-board pattern, no mascot, no watermark. Real alpha transparency outside the rounded tile, not a checkerboard baked into the pixels.

## Wordmark first pass

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: production horizontal brand logo for Frost/Byte desktop application. Input image is a reference for brand identity: reuse the same angular interlocking FB emblem with its distinctive diagonal slash, electric lime facets and pale icy highlights. Create a new horizontal logo lockup on a genuinely transparent background. Place a compact version of this emblem on the left, WITHOUT the rounded-square dark app tile, with a finely typeset wordmark to the right. Exact main text: "FROST/BYTE". Typeface: original-looking clean geometric uppercase sans serif, moderately wide, precise and contemporary, easily readable; FROST in icy off-white #E8EDE8, slash in electric lime #22D511, BYTE in slightly softer off-white. A small secondary line below the wordmark says exactly "by Blackfrost_AI" in muted sage grey. Keep emblem, wordmark, and subline perfectly optically aligned; wide generous spacing between emblem and wordmark. Horizontal composition around 3:1, artwork fills most of the canvas with small consistent transparent safety margins. Emblem is simpler and flatter than app icon but retains the recognizable geometry and lime brand color. Refined software identity, crisp typography, no extra symbols, no extra words, no border, no scene, no mockup, no tile behind the emblem, no watermark. Transparency must be alpha, never a checkerboard printed in the artwork.

## Final wordmark edit

Use case: precise-object-edit. Edit the supplied Frost/Byte logo into a pristine production logo. Preserve the exact interlocking lime FB/slash emblem on the left and the exact main text FROST/BYTE to the right. Preserve the secondary text by Blackfrost_AI below the wordmark. Change the backdrop to a completely solid, uniform dark graphite #121512 across the entire canvas, with NO transparency. Repair ALL distressed, grainy, chipped, noisy, speckled edges and interiors: the letters must be solid smooth icy-white shapes with perfectly clean sharp anti-aliased edges. The lime mark must have broad smooth geometric facets and completely clean edges, no holes or scraps. Keep a clean premium geometric sans-serif wordmark. Main wordmark white with lime slash, secondary line small muted sage grey. Wide 3:1 composition, centered lockup with even generous margins. Flat straight-on brand artwork, minimal and professionally typeset, no extra text, no effects, no scene. This is a clean software brand lockup, absolutely no grunge or distress anywhere.
