WireGuard overlays

Existing tunnels stay managed by WireGuard.

Adding an overlay saves its name so you can check an exact peer address. FrostByte never reads private keys, imports configurations, starts tunnels, or changes VPN routes. This is not a torrent VPN binding or kill switch; connections follow operating-system routes.

Find a device

Leave the address blank to check up to one local /24 for SSH and file sharing. WireGuard always requires an exact peer address; overlay ranges are never swept.

Active bandwidth · this computer

Your mode is saved across restarts. These controls affect this computer, not Spark or other devices. Unlimited removes software rate and queue caps; network speed, available peers, and hardware still matter.

Global limits (KB/s · 0 = unlimited)

Caps apply to the whole client instantly. Set an upload cap if sharing competes with anything else on the line.

Alternative limits active now

Windows that cross midnight belong to the day they start on. Normal mode ignores this schedule until you explicitly select Scheduled again. Turning ALT on from the status bar asks for confirmation.

Queueing

Pulls beyond the limit are parked as queued and resume on their own as slots free. Pausing something yourself keeps it paused.

There is no active-item queue limit for completed seeds or models you explicitly share. Peer connection limits are separate. Settings here apply to this computer only.

Seeding goals

Goals stop things this box pulled — models you explicitly share from My Models are never auto-stopped. Stopped files stay on disk.

Where models go

Pulled models land here. Sharing works from anywhere on disk.

Moves the model folder after it finishes. Cross-volume moves fall back to copy-then-delete.

Catalog source

Use a public HTTPS JSON list. Private-network addresses are blocked; imports are limited to 2 MB and 2,000 entries.

Access token

No token set.

Needed for gated models (Llama, Gemma…). Create one at huggingface.co/settings/tokens with read access. The token is validated against whoami, stored with OS-level encryption where available, and only ever sent to huggingface.co as a Bearer header.

Gated models

Gated repos (auto or manual) need both a token and accepted access on the model page. A 401 means no token; a 403 means the token is fine but access wasn't granted — we link you to the request page when that happens.

Model cards

Model cards are rendered on this computer as plain text. No scripts or embedded pages run. FrostByte fetches images on your behalf from Hugging Face. Images hosted on other sites load only when you click them, because loading them shares your IP address with that site. Turn this off to show no images.

Session

Your app. Your data.

FrostByte does not collect analytics, diagnostics, or automatic feedback. There is no reporting switch to enable. Your library, settings, and device history stay on your devices.

Peer-to-peer transfers reveal your network address to peers. Services you choose to use, including Hugging Face, update checks, and GitHub, receive ordinary connection information.

Tell us what could work better

Feedback is optional and goes through GitHub. Nothing is attached or submitted by FrostByte. You choose what to post; GitHub issues are public.

Terms and privacy

FrostByte Account & Sign-In

Not signed in. Local downloads and HuggingFace model fit checks work without an account. Sign in to use FrostNet Plus/Pro features.

Email/password, account creation, password reset, and Google sign-in all happen on frostbyte.si in your system browser. FrostByte never receives your password; the website passes back a short-lived one-time code and your session is encrypted in the OS keychain.

Public profile

Your handle is your public alias on FrostNet (creators stay identified by publisher key). Display name and bio appear on your creator page. Changes are saved to your FrostByte account and confirmed by the Hub.

Publisher binding

Sign in and unlock your publisher identity to bind it to this account. Binding proves you own the key — the Hub issues a one-time challenge signed on this device.

FrostNet Connection & API Token

FrostByte is pinned to the live production FrostNet Hub API. There is no localhost fallback and no editable Hub URL. FrostNet community browsing is coming soon; meanwhile Search (Hugging Face) needs no account. Offline bundle seeding needs no account either: import a signed bundle seed from a NAS or community mirror.

Tier: —

Plus and Pro are granted by your FrostByte account and confirmed by the Hub server. They cannot be switched on locally. Offline bundle seeding is always free.

Follow

Follow creators by publisher pubkey (64 hex). Your list is local; the Hub validates on publish. Creator profiles and feeds arrive with FrostNet — following never auto-downloads; you still review every transfer.
